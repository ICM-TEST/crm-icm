# Setup questionnaire

Collect these from the practice before setup. Every answer is a value that goes onto their Drive, never into this repo.

## Staff
1. Each staff member who will use the system: full name as it should appear on the log, and the Mac they use. Fill `Staff 1` to `Staff 4`, in alphabetical order. (`lists/names.md`)
2. Each staff member's Google account, to share `CLAUDE WORKSPACE FILES` with. (Drive sharing)
3. That Google Drive for desktop is installed on each Mac and `CLAUDE WORKSPACE FILES` has synced there. (The project instruction names the folder, so no path is needed.)

## Practitioners and offices
4. The tax practitioners, up to two, as their names should be shown. Fill `Practitioner 1` and `Practitioner 2`. (`lists/names.md`)
5. The offices, up to two. Fill `Office 1` and `Office 2`. (`lists/names.md`)

## Administration
6. Who is the practice administrator: the one who merges Drive conflict copies and edits `names.md` when the system provider says so?

## The old CRM
7. What do they use today, and can it export their clients (a spreadsheet, a file, a report)? Ask for one sample export. (Moving clients over is the TODO in [onboarding.md](onboarding.md).)

Roles are not decided yet: every staff member has the same access.
