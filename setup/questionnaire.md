# Setup questionnaire

Collect these from the practice before setup. Every answer is a value that goes onto their Drive, never into this repo.

## The practice
1. The practice's name, as their Claude should say it. (Project instruction only)

## Staff
2. Each staff member who will use the system: full name as it should appear on the log, and the Mac they use. Fill `Staff 1` to `Staff 4`, in alphabetical order. (`lists/names.md`)
3. Each staff member's Google account, to share `CLAUDE WORKSPACE FILES` with. (Drive sharing)
4. That Google Drive for desktop is installed on each Mac and `CLAUDE WORKSPACE FILES` has synced there. (The project instruction names the folder, so no path is needed.)

## Practitioners and offices
5. The tax practitioners, up to two, as their names should be shown. Fill `Practitioner 1` and `Practitioner 2`. (`lists/names.md`)
6. The offices, up to two. Fill `Office 1` and `Office 2`. (`lists/names.md`)

## Administration
7. Who is the practice administrator: the one who edits `names.md` when the system provider says so, and checks the `merged/` folder if a merge is ever questioned?

## The old CRM
8. What do they use today, and can it export their clients as CSV? Who at the practice can run the exports? (Moving clients over: [population-guide.md](population-guide.md).)
9. A switch day: the day the old CRM stops being changed and the load runs.

Roles are not decided yet: every staff member has the same access.
